# Data

## RDD analysis — Uruguay PANES

**Source:** Survey data from Manacorda, Miguel & Vigorito (2011), "Government Transfers and Political Support,"
*American Economic Journal: Applied Economics* 3(3), 1–28.

The dataset covers households within ±0.02 of the PANES income score eligibility cutoff,
surveyed at programme start (2005), during the programme (2007), and one year post-programme (2008).

**Access:** The dataset is not independently redistributable. The original paper's replication
files are available at the AEJ: Applied Economics data archive.

## DiD analysis — Victoria COVID-19 lockdown

**Source:** Household, Income and Labour Dynamics in Australia (HILDA) Survey.
Managed by the Melbourne Institute of Applied Economic and Social Research.
20 waves, 215,733 observations, 16,513 individuals.

**Access:** HILDA requires a data access agreement with the Melbourne Institute.
See: <https://melbourneinstitute.unimelb.edu.au/hilda>
