Selected quantitative research

A Test for the Practical Relevance of Endogeneity in Semiparametric Distribution Regression

MSc Thesis  ·  University of Cologne  ·  2026  ·  Supervised by Prof. Dr. Dominik Wied

Novel asymptotically pivotal test based on L²-distance between naive and IV/control-function estimators, validated via Monte Carlo simulation in R. Applied to Card (1995) returns-to-education data: IV correction is relevant only for high-education workers — a heterogeneous finding invisible to scalar endogeneity tests.

Statistical Theory Distribution Regression Monte Carlo R

Distribution Sensitive Policy Analysis based on WELLBYs

MSc Dissertation  ·  LSE  ·  2024  ·  Supervised by Dr. Christian Krekel

Difference-in-differences with propensity score matching on Australia's HILDA panel (n > 17,000) to estimate the distributional wellbeing impact of Victoria's COVID-19 lockdown, revealing disproportionate effects on the worst-off.

The Effect of Cash Transfers on Political Support

Policy Analysis Essay  ·  LSE  ·  2024  ·  Distinction (83%)

Regression discontinuity design (McCrary test, donut-hole robustness checks, bandwidth sensitivity analysis) applied to Uruguay's PANES cash transfer programme. Finds a robust ~10% causal increase in political support.
